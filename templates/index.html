FORMULATION & MIXING

Turn approved ingredients
into controlled batches.

Formulation is treated as a disciplined production stage: approved inputs, documented batch work, controlled processing and traceable review. The system records what is authorized rather than inventing recipes.

01 · RAW INPUTS

Material verification

Record incoming materials, lot information, units, status and release before production use.

02 · FORMULATION

Batch preparation

Track approved formulation references, planned quantity, batch identifiers and process checkpoints.

03 · MIXING

Controlled processing

Document equipment, operators, start and finish times, observations and authorized process notes.

04 · REVIEW

Batch disposition

Route samples and records into testing and quality review before anything is marked as released.
